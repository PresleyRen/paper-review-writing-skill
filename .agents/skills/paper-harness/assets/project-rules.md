## Academic paper workflow

For research writing, preserve claim meaning, evidence status, technical terminology and author emphasis. Read the project's writing rules when available. Keep editorial commentary outside manuscript prose.

Route by the requested action: paper-positioning for argument/design, paper-draft for rough notes, paper-voice for existing prose, paper-rewrite for source-based rewriting, paper-redteam for strict review, paper-evidence-audit for source/number checks and paper-revision for acting on comments. Use paper-harness for a multi-stage task. Load companion skills only if actually available.

For review, require a located basis, consequence, evidence status, smallest decisive check and closure condition. Distinguish confirmed defects, suspicions, missing evidence and preferences. Do not invent rejection reasons or claim completed experiments from plans.

For full revision, track claims and material issues outside the manuscript; recheck only affected dependencies. A local workflow status or lint script does not establish scientific correctness or guarantee acceptance.
