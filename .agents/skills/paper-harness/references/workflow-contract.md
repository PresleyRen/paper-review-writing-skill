# Workflow contract

## The research object drives the work

Track a claim through what the paper says, which evidence supports it, what could defeat the inference, and how the manuscript changes after review.

A root issue may affect the abstract, a results paragraph, a figure caption and a conclusion. Treat these as dependencies of one issue, not four independent rejection reasons.

## Stages and exit criteria

| Stage | Required work | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Locate the requested deliverable, source material and authorized scope. | Input inventory and actual task scope. |
| Positioning, when needed | State the claim, closest relevant work, competing explanation and discriminating check. | A bounded claim or a specific evidence task. |
| Drafting | Convert supplied evidence and author notes into the requested prose. | Text with preserved claim meaning and evidence status. |
| Reviewing | Examine the central inference and relevant implementation/evaluation conditions. | Located issues with basis, consequence and closure conditions. |
| Revising | Implement supported repairs and record unresolved evidence needs. | Concrete edits and justified issue dispositions. |
| Auditing | Check the current claims, numbers, units, citations and related occurrences. | Traceable check results; unavailable sources remain unverified. |
| Ready | Confirm requested scope is complete and material issues are resolved. | Current records and affected artifacts rechecked. |

“Ready” is an internal project status. It does not mean a real reviewer will accept the paper.

## Three useful review passes

1. Argument: Does the conclusion follow from the stated premises and observations? What alternative explanation remains?
2. Evidence and operation: Do code, data, metrics, budgets and logs implement the claimed comparison?
3. Text: Does the manuscript communicate the supported conclusion with the author's emphasis and necessary scope?

Use independent agents if available and useful. Otherwise make these separate passes. Do not call them independent external validation when the same agent performed them.

## Issue resolution

- Correct an erroneous quantity from the underlying record and update every affected occurrence.
- Add a completed, traced control when it was actually authorized and run.
- Narrow or withdraw an unsupported claim, recording that disposition.
- Reject an invalid review premise with a source or a directly checkable argument.
- Leave a material missing-evidence issue open when none of the above applies.

Adding “may” or moving a generic disclaimer cannot close an invalid metric or absent control.
A claimed closure needs a concrete revision or check and its result.

## Stop conditions

Do not run unlimited review loops. Recheck changed claims and dependencies.
An unchanged resolved issue needs new evidence before reopening.
After completing supported edits, an unavailable source or unrun experiment remains a precise blocker; it does not justify inventing results.

## Standalone use

Without the project files, maintain a compact prose record and use the smallest applicable stages.
Do not require every optional ledger or companion skill to exist before helping.
