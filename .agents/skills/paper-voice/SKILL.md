# Paper Voice

Revise existing manuscript prose so that the research and the author's thinking remain visible.
Make the smallest changes that improve the requested passage.

## Establish the editing contract

Read the passage and requested scope: grammar only, paragraph flow, author voice, concision, or structural revision.
Read the project-root ACADEMIC_WRITING.md if present.
Use relevant approved author samples and the current draft. If no suitable sample exists, follow the stated preferences without claiming an exact style match.

A request to reduce “AI味” or defensive writing activates this skill for language quality. It does not make an AI detector a ground-truth author identifier.

## Preserve a meaning fingerprint

Preserve who or what is discussed, the comparator, outcome, direction, magnitude, population, setting, uncertainty, negations, citation attachment and evidence status.

Keep technical terms stable. State, memory and context are not casual synonyms. Neither are accuracy, reliability and robustness.

Language editing can preserve author-supplied claims without independently certifying them. A concrete contradiction that prevents a faithful edit calls for a targeted check or separate note; ordinary uncertainty does not require a new audit.

## Remove editorial contamination

Identify sentences that describe the assistant, this revision, anticipated reviewer reactions, persuasive strategy or generic modesty. Remove them from manuscript prose or move a material issue to the review record.

Keep actual research rationale, substantive trade-offs, negative results and necessary scope conditions.

Use references/claim-repair.md when deciding whether a phrase needs deletion, a narrower claim, a direct statement or real evidence.

Do not ban may, only, not, although, suggests or similar words. Determine whether they carry scientific meaning in that sentence. Do not replace careful wording with stronger causal or universal language merely to sound confident.

## Keep natural information flow

- Preserve the author's useful words and emphasis.
- Let the section determine paragraph structure; do not require every paragraph to open with a thesis and end with a summary.
- Put familiar context where it helps readers locate a new point, without mechanically enforcing a sentence template.
- Use active or passive voice according to the subject being discussed.
- Keep coherent shifts of emphasis, different sentence lengths and occasional compact statements.
- Do not add errors, random inversions, repeated colloquialisms or artificial “burstiness.”
- Prefer precise ordinary English over inflated academic vocabulary.
- Do not invent personal experience, experimental surprises or first-person judgments unsupported by the author's material.

## Perform one proportional check

Compare the revision with the original for changed facts, numbers, signs, conditions, causal verbs, certainty, citations and technical terms.
Confirm that necessary limitations remain near the claims they qualify.
Check whether a clear original sentence was changed for no useful reason; retain it when it was better.
Stop after the requested scope is satisfied. Reopen a resolved issue only when a relevant change or new evidence warrants it.

## Output

For “only the revised text,” deliver manuscript prose alone when it can be completed faithfully.
Give a minimal separate note only for a material unresolved blocker.
For a requested explanation or exercise, explain the few changes that affect meaning or emphasis.
Do not attach routine self-evaluation, detector claims or an unsolicited full-paper review.
