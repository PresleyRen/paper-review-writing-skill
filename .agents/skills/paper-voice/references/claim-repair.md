# Repair claims and preserve voice

Use these examples as editing decisions, not lexical bans.
The examples are generic or fictional, not evidence about the author's work.

| Draft problem | Appropriate repair | What remains necessary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “To avoid overclaiming, we cautiously report that…” | State the supported result directly. | The relevant evaluation setting and uncertainty. |
| “It is important to note that our method is not perfect.” | Remove the generic sentence. | A specific failure or trade-off if the evidence contains one. |
| “The mechanism explains the gain,” but no mechanism test exists. | Report the gain; present the mechanism as a useful hypothesis or remove it. | The missing test belongs in review/planning notes when needed. |
| “Despite losing on accuracy, our method remains superior…” | State the observed accuracy and the separately measured cost or latency trade-off. | Comparable definitions and the unfavorable result. |
| “The controller is designed to recover…” with only a specification. | Keep the design status; shorten surrounding padding. | Do not turn it into implemented behavior or measured reliability. |
| “The controller may possibly perhaps…” with verified execution behavior. | Describe the observed execution or established implementation directly. | The scope of what was verified. |
| “The analysis does not distinguish X from Y.” | Keep it when those explanations affect interpretation. | This is a substantive uncertainty, not automatic defensiveness. |

## Necessary boundaries

“The agent completed 74 of 100 simulated tasks in one run” carries a setting, denominator and replication limit. Do not erase these to create a stronger tone. A section that can be read independently may need its own brief qualification.

A measured result can support an explicit trade-off. The author does not have to apologize for a valid observation. Conversely, moving every limitation to the end of the paper can leave a misleading abstract.

## A source of repeated defensive prose

A review may say:

> Extra model calls could explain the reported gain. The same-budget control is missing.

A manuscript repair might report the actual budgets and narrow the mechanism claim.
A research task might add the missing comparison.
A generic “results should be interpreted cautiously” sentence does not perform either repair.

Keep issue IDs, reviewer voice and closure reasoning outside manuscript text.
An issue closed by withdrawing a claim has not been closed by demonstrating that claim.

## Style sample protocol

Record, only when useful for repeated writing:

- Which supplied or approved samples were used and which genre they represent.
- The author's typical placement of important information.
- Useful patterns of first person, terminology, paragraph development and transitions.
- Choices the author explicitly disliked.
- One short grounded example per pattern.

Treat the profile as editable guidance. Do not turn observed tendencies into required quotas. A polished introduction and a working research note need different rhythms.
