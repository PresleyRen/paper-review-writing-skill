# Paper Rewrite

Reconstruct source-based prose around the author's current argumentative purpose.
Preserve attribution, meaning and the distinctions between different sources.

## Read the actual material

Identify the passage to revise, its role in the paper and any available source text or similarity report.
Read the project-root ACADEMIC_WRITING.md if it exists.
Consult references/source-rewrite-protocol.md for source comparison or requested alternatives.

If only the author's passage is supplied, improve its structure while retaining existing citations. Do not claim that source accuracy, corpus overlap or a detector outcome has been checked.

When source support is central, open the original paper or supplied excerpt. A title, search snippet or citation key alone does not establish support.

## Separate the quantities

A similarity report measures matching text under the product's corpus and settings.
An AI writing indicator has a product-specific interpretation and can contain errors.
A generation-time text watermark is a different provenance mechanism.

Interpret a supplied report according to its actual product and documentation. Do not describe every score as the probability that the author is an AI. Do not guarantee a threshold, a watermark outcome or “human” certification.

Spend the task on faithful original writing, appropriate quotation, synthesis and attribution. Do not manufacture mistakes, unusual punctuation or fake personal history to influence a score.

## Preserve the semantic record

Extract the source's actual proposition, evidence type, conditions, uncertainty and limits relevant to the passage.
Keep quotations visibly quoted and cited when exact wording is necessary.
Retain conventional method names, definitions and technical terms when substitution would change meaning.
Preserve the citation's connection to the supported claim.

Keep source-specific findings separate from the current paper's results. Do not turn “prior work reported” into “we demonstrate.”

## Rebuild from the proposition

Write a fresh account from the meaning record and the current paragraph's purpose.
Then compare with the source for both excessive phrase borrowing and altered meaning.

Useful reorganizations include:

- Begin with the result when the reader needs the finding first.
- Begin with the method when the procedure is the paragraph's subject.
- Group several sources by a substantive agreement, difference or unresolved question.
- Split a sentence that compresses different claims, or combine redundancy without losing attribution.

Active voice, passive voice and result-first order are options, not a required sequence. Do not select the least conventional version just because it differs.

For a requested exercise, offer up to the requested number of genuinely distinct versions and explain their different emphasis. Do not produce three cosmetic synonym substitutions.

## Check and deliver

Check subject, comparator, direction, magnitude, negation, scope, uncertainty and citation attachment against the source and original.
Retain relevant unfavorable findings.
A lower overlap score does not erase attribution obligations.
Only report a changed score if a specific authorized tool actually measured both versions under stated settings.

For a manuscript-only revision, output the revised passage. For report analysis, give the matched location, why it matches, an appropriate action and remaining source checks.
Do not add detector commentary to the manuscript.
